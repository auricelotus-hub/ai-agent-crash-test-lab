FAQ

Questions buyers will ask.

Is this a security certification?

No. It is a testing framework and risk-signal system. It cannot prove an agent is secure in every environment.

Do I need coding experience?

No. The buyer-facing workflow is designed around copy/paste tests, observation, evidence capture and a scorecard.

Does it block an AI agent automatically?

No. The product helps you identify behaviors and define better boundaries. Technical enforcement depends on the AI platform and setup you use.

Can I use it with any AI agent?

The scenarios are platform-agnostic, but some tests may need adaptation depending on whether the agent has tools, browser access, email access or other capabilities.
